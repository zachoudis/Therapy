Systemic individual therapy in Berlin

My services are for people who feel stuck, overwhelmed, or are going through a personal crisis. How we perceive ourselves and relate to ourselves is often shaped by beliefs and experiences from our family of origin. What used to be helpful or necessary in the past can limit us today and make change harder. That’s why we look together at your relationships with other people as well as your relationship with yourself.

Common reasons to start (examples)

  • Stress, overwhelm, and burnout prevention
  • Relationship patterns and boundaries
  • Life transitions and decision-making
  • Self-worth and emotional regulation
  • Confidence, identity, and values clarification

What you can expect

A calm, structured process focused on resources, new perspectives, and practical steps that fit your everyday life.

How sessions work (structured)

  1. Clarify goals and current situation
  2. Understand patterns (relationships, context, stressors)
  3. Develop options and small experiments
  4. Integrate learning and track progress
