Systemic couple therapy in Berlin

In relationships we often find ourselves in recurring—and sometimes distressing—dynamics that are difficult to change alone. I support you in recognizing stuck patterns, understanding each other’s inner experience, and changing interactions step by step, working with the Emotionally Focused Therapy approach (EFT).

Who it’s for

Couples who want to improve communication, rebuild trust, or navigate a transition. Sessions can be helpful in acute conflict as well as for preventive relationship care.

Typical topics (examples)

  • Recurring conflicts and communication patterns
  • Rebuilding trust and emotional safety
  • Transitions (parenthood, moving, work stress)
  • Separation support and respectful closure
  • Intimacy, closeness–distance, and unmet needs

How sessions work (structured process)

  1. Clarify goals: what should be different after therapy?
  2. Understand patterns: what happens in the cycle between you?
  3. Practice new steps: concrete communication and repair moves
  4. Transfer: small experiments between sessions

FAQ

How many sessions do couples usually need?

Some couples benefit from a short series (e.g., 4–8 sessions), others prefer longer support depending on the topic and stress level.

Do we need to come together?

Usually yes. In some situations, one or two individual sessions can be useful to clarify goals or context.

Is couple therapy covered by insurance?

In many private practices in Germany, couple therapy is not covered by statutory health insurance. Please contact us to confirm current options.
